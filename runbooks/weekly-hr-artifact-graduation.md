# Weekly HR Artifact Graduation

Updated: 2026-05-22

## Decision

Graduate the repeated AI-in-HR artifact review into a runbook.

Do not make it an autobrowse target yet.

## Why

This past week repeated the same workflow:

1. Capture practical HR/AI field notes.
2. Identify one small reusable artifact.
3. Strip out employer-specific or confidential detail.
4. Convert the idea into a buyer-readable OpenClaw-HR pattern, use case, or checklist.
5. Decide whether it belongs in the public workflow repo, private wiki, or personal coaching notes.

The workflow is valuable because it turns practitioner-developed workflow learning into reusable OpenClaw-HR proof points.

It should stay a runbook because the key decisions are editorial and risk-based:

- Is this public-source or sanitized enough?
- Is it useful to HR operators?
- Does it overstate what AI should do in HR?
- Does it need legal, privacy, or human-review boundaries?
- Is it worth publishing, or should it stay private?

Those calls should not be automated.

## Inputs

Use only approved inputs:

- public AI-in-HR research
- public HR technology or compliance examples
- fully synthetic ExampleCo scenarios
- original practitioner reflections
- existing local OpenClaw-HR drafts

Do not use:

- current or former employer confidential information
- real employee, manager, or candidate details
- internal company documents
- identifiable workplace situations
- proprietary employer systems or data

## Weekly checklist

1. Review the past week's notes for repeated AI-in-HR themes.
2. Pick one candidate artifact.
3. Classify it:
   - public repo
   - private wiki
   - personal coaching note
   - discard
4. Apply the HR Writing Quality Gate.
5. Add explicit boundaries:
   - human review
   - privacy
   - legal/compliance escalation
   - source requirements
6. Choose one output format:
   - pattern
   - use case
   - runbook
   - checklist
   - demo script
7. Keep the artifact small enough to read in under five minutes.
8. Update source-of-truth docs if the artifact moves.

## Publish criteria

A public artifact should pass all of these:

- no employer-confidential facts
- no identifiable people or workplace situations
- useful to an HR operator without extra context
- clear human-review boundaries
- not framed as legal advice
- specific enough to be more than AI hype
- aligned with the repo's auditability and human-in-the-loop thesis

## When to consider autobrowse later

Only consider autobrowse if the public-web portion becomes repetitive and low-risk, such as:

- checking a fixed set of public HR/AI sources for new posts
- collecting titles and links only
- producing a non-published candidate list for human review

Even then, autobrowse should stop before writing or publishing. The graduation decision remains human-reviewed.
